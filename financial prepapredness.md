### The full cost of studying abroad

Exchange rates affect what a family actually pays across a multi-year program. A budget built in rupees today will shift as the rupee moves against the dollar, pound, or Australian dollar.

- Tuition fees
- Accommodation and meals
- Health insurance
- Visa application and renewal fees
- Application and enrollment fees
- Travel costs, initial and recurring
- Books and course materials

---

### Visa applications require documented proof of financial ability

Every major destination country requires liquid funds in a bank account, held for a minimum period before the application date, covering at least the first year of tuition and living expenses. Funds that appear shortly before the application date raise concerns with visa officers. The specific amount depends on the country, city, and program.

Indian banks issue education loan sanction letters accepted as financial proof in most countries. A sanction letter can substitute for or supplement liquid funds. Obtaining one takes time and requires documentation the lending institution specifies. Families exploring this route need to begin well before the application deadline.

### Scholarships

| Merit-based | Need-based |
|---|---|
| Based on academic performance, test scores, and extracurricular record. Eligibility thresholds vary by institution and country. | Based on documented family financial position. Available amounts vary and are not always publicly listed. |

CFC works with participating institutions to help students assess realistic scholarship options before committing time to applications.

---

### Online programs

Tuition is generally lower and visa-related financial requirements do not apply. International tuition payments still require foreign currency capability. Not all Indian bank accounts handle this without prior setup, and arranging a forex card, international credit card, or wire transfer can take longer than students expect.
